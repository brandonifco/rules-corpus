# rules-corpus 1.0.0

Given retained authoritative source artifacts, rules-corpus reproducibly produces and verifies
a portable, immutable, addressable corpus whose content identity, derivation history and source
mappings are explicit, without containing or interpreting any domain rules.

## What ships

- **The corpus format**, schema `rules-corpus/manifest/1` and `rules-corpus/build/1`
  ([corpus-format.md](../corpus-format.md)).
- **The `rules-corpus` command** (`init`, `import`, `build`, `verify`, `inspect`, `diff`,
  `pack`), with `--json` on every command and exit codes 0 to 3.
- **Two adapters**: `text` 1 (line, block and heading segmentation, page markers) and `xml` 1
  (one segment per named element). `ICorpusAdapter` is demonstrated by both, run over two real
  consumers' sources ([M5](../calibration/2026-10-01-m5-xml.md)).
- **The libraries** `RulesCorpus`, `RulesCorpus.Adapters.Text` and `RulesCorpus.Adapters.Xml`
  (net8.0; net10.0), with their public surface baselined in `PublicAPI.Shipped.txt`
  ([decision 0012](../decisions/0012-the-1-0-public-surface.md)).

## The contract

[compatibility.md](../compatibility.md) states what a consumer may rely on and what changing
each part costs.

## Evidence

- `./scripts/validate.sh full` passes: build and tests on net8.0 and net10.0, three committed
  sample corpora rebuilt byte for byte and packed twice to identical archives, and the
  repository invariants.
- Both real consumers calibrate against this tree with `tools/calibration/run.sh`: the pinned
  baselines project exactly, `verify --rebuild` gives the expected exit, and a second build is
  byte-identical. faa-part-107 segments into 61 sections.

## Known and deferred

Hardening and one design question, none a blocker, are in the [backlog](../backlog.md): limits
validation, packed-corpus total size, `diff` not verifying, stack traces for unexpected
exception types, and pack atomicity under a write failure. The adapters' determinism holds on
one .NET runtime; across runtimes it holds for the inputs they document.
